# ROZMERY 4K — handoff notes

Technical notes for whoever continues this demo (written for Claude Code).
The user-facing description in Slovak is in `README.md`. ROZMERY 4K is the
8K demo in `../8k` squeezed into 4096 bytes per file, at the owner's request
("try and fit this 8K demo into 4K, basically make it a 4K demo"). It reuses
the platform layers and tools of the 4K intro BRÁNA (repository root) and of
the 8K; it does not modify any of their files.

## 1. State

| File | Size | Margin | Tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dist/rozmery4k-windows.exe` | 3681 B | 415 B | Wine 9.0 + llvmpipe: whole demo, exits by itself, visuals correct, 31 of 33 checked audio windows sample-exact (the others hold Wine's playback gaps under full CPU load) |
| `dist/rozmery4k-linux` | 4084 B | 12 B | Xvfb + llvmpipe + PipeWire: whole demo, exits after 201 s, ESC, visuals correct, audio sample-exact in 39 of 39 windows (the recording skipped 1024 samples twice under load) |
| `dist/rozmery4k-macos` | 4035 B | 61 B | `../tools/macsim` (its machine code on Linux): whole demo, audio bit-identical over the whole song |

- The limit is 4096 bytes per file (`build.sh` checks it). **Linux binds**:
  its payload is roughly (the two minified shaders compressed together) +
  1485 B, macOS + 1435 B. Every byte of shader must be paid for on Linux.
- **Never run on real hardware** (no real GPU, Windows or Mac in the cloud
  environment it was made in). The owner has an NVIDIA RTX 3060 PC (CachyOS,
  2560x1440) and an Intel MacBook with Intel HD Graphics.
- Length: 104 bars at 125 BPM = 199.68 s, as the 8K (`song.sh`).
- Platform layers: Linux and macOS are copies of BRÁNA's (`../src/linux`,
  `../src/mac`) with other shaders and the speed test at `T_HEAVY` = 182.4 s
  (bar 95, the heaviest moment); macOS has no `SCALE` in the release (as
  BRÁNA; `-DSCALE` adds about 210 B). Windows is the 8K's `src/win/main.c`
  (scaling and the music rendered in 16 bands; Crinkler has room).

## 4. Visuals (`src/scene.frag`)

One raymarching shader, `u = (time, width, height, 0)`.

- **map():** ground plane `p.y` (absent while `|B - 85| < 12`, i.e. bars
  73-97, when the camera is near or inside the bulb and the ground would show
  through its gaps); the building (bars < 63 and clock `T > 4`): 15 columns
  (`clamp(round(..))` repetition), slabs every 3 units up to the current
  height, and the glass as a **solid** block one floor below the top (a thin
  shell was thinner than the march tolerance and its normals flipped: light
  scribbles on the glass); the Mandelbulb (power 8, trig form, `sph()` reused
  for the polar step, orbit trap `G` for its colours).
- **Materials** are told apart after the march: ground `p.y < .001 * t` (the
  hit tolerance; a fixed `.01` made rings at a distance), glass by its
  position, bulb by the bar, concrete otherwise. Lit rooms: a hash of the
  floor and the column of 1-unit rooms. The blueprint grid is emissive
  (`exp(-t*.05)` fade, visible while the clock `T < 10`, i.e. at the start
  and again after the rewind) — it glows as a neon floor at night.
- **Shading:** sun + sky ambient, soft shadow (28 steps, no early exit: that
  saved 10 B and costs nothing at the heaviest moments, see 8), distance fog
  scaled by the view scale `Z`, tone map `sqrt(1 - exp(-1.4 c))`.
- **Sky:** horizon relative to the camera's up vector `U` (global), so in the
  flight the horizon follows the bulb's surface (with world y it stood up as a
  vertical band).
- **Tesseract:** 16 vertices `sign(fract(i / vec4(2,4,8,16)) - .4) * A`,
  rotated in xw (from bar 46), perspective from 4D, projected to the screen
  (`* M` with `M = mat3(right, up, forward)`); 32 edges + 16 vertex dots as
  glow by the distance of the pixel to the projected segment, depth-tested
  against the march. `A` animates the extrusions: the building's frame (7.5,
  9.4, 5.5) collapses dimension by dimension (37, 37.5, 38), then the point
  grows (38, 40, 42, 44) and collapses at 62-63.5.

### 4.1 The camera and the flight

- Bars 0-30: an orbit around the building, descending, looking at its
  growing top.
- From bar 30 one path, `fly(t)` = (latitude, longitude, radius / 5) around
  the centre C = (0, 9.6, 0): in from the dusk (radius 40 -> 16.3, latitude
  1.69 -> the equator, bars 30-40), an orbit at the **equator** at radius 16.3
  (the tesseract and the bulb sit on the horizon over the grid), the dive
  (70-78, `F = smoothstep(70, 78)`), the flight (78-95), the climb-out
  (95-100, `F` falls back). Longitude `1.727 - .0306 t` throughout.
- Radius: `.8 + exp(mix(.9 + 1.07 e, h(t), F))` with the flight's log-height
  `h(t) = -2.54 + .274 cos(.72 t + 1.006) + .351 smoothstep(92.5, 94.5, t)`;
  latitude in the flight `1.5708 + .06 smoothstep(77, 83, t)` (veers south off
  the causeway into the valley band).
- `h(t)` is **fitted** by `tools/flypath.py design`: it maps the bulb's
  surface radius over (latitude, longitude) around the route (marching in
  from radius 1.3), takes the highest surface within ~0.006 rad and 0.3 bar
  of each point + 0.004, and fits the formula from below (least squares with
  a heavy weight on points under the curve, then raised to clear all). Mean
  height above the terrain 0.025 bulb units. The bulb has a 7-fold symmetry
  in longitude (2π/7) and a mirror symmetry, so the route's longitude can be
  shifted by multiples of 0.8976.
- View: `mix(toward C, ahead, F)` where "ahead" is the path 0.35 bar ahead
  made **level** (radial part removed) and tilted down by `-.2 v`; up =
  `mix((0,1,0), v, F)` (v: radial). Two lessons:
  - With latitude changing during the dive (the first design dove from
    latitude 1.39 to the equator), "ahead" pointed steeply down in world
    space and the up vector nearly opposed the view: the camera rolled at
    ~100°/bar. Diving at constant latitude (orbit at the equator) fixed it.
  - With the look-ahead following the path's climb, the climb-out looked into
    the empty sky (the climb dwarfs the forward motion). Level look-ahead
    fixed it and keeps the flight calm.
- `tools/flypath.py` (check): closest approach 0.00188 bulb units (bar 79.3,
  the distance estimate is a lower bound), the view turns at most 30°/bar,
  the up vector 38°/bar (the 8K: 45°/bar).

## 5. Music (`src/synth.frag`)

One fragment per two stereo samples; `i = int(dot(gl_FragCoord.xy - .5,
vec2(2, 2048)))` (exact in float up to 2^24). 104 bars, 8-bar phrases from
bar 6 with a level each (`0x1F4E4E4 >> p*2 & 3`, `p = (bar + 2) >> 3`): 0 pad
+ arp, 1 + kick, bass, 8th hats, 2 + snare, 16th hats, 3 + hook.

- `voice()`: a band-limited saw (discrete summation formula) with attack,
  decay and a release at the note's end; phase reduced to one period.
- **Chords** without a table: chord tone k of the 2-bar unit u is
  `((root * 12 + k * 24 + 5) / 7 + 48)` — stacked thirds in C major (the
  integer formula `(d * 12 + 5) / 7` gives the major scale for any degree d).
  Roots for (u & 3) + 4 * (tesseract part) packed in `0x21355243` (F G E A,
  A F D E), C from bar 98. The building's chords match the 8K's except G7
  for G6; the tesseract's are plain 7ths (the 8K: Am9 Fmaj7#11 Dm9 E7sus4).
- Kick with a pitch drop and side-chain ducking of the tonal parts; snare
  with a gated tail on 2 and 4; hats (16ths at level >= 2); booms (37, 37.5,
  38 deeper and deeper, 64); noise risers into bars 6, 46, 78 (`0x221` over
  phrases); bass in 8ths with the octave on the "and"; pad (12 detuned
  voices, 0.11 s fades at chord changes); arpeggio (2, 4, 8, 16 notes per bar
  while the tesseract grows in bars 38-46, 16ths otherwise) and the hook,
  both with a dotted-eighth echo (4 taps); hook = 25 entries of
  `(pitch - 75) * 8 + eighths - 1`.
- **Bug found in this round:** the booms' selector made `exp(-2.5 y)` with
  `y` far below 0 for bars 38.5-63 — the whole tesseract part was a clipped
  wall of noise (rms 1.0). Fixed (`x = B > 64. ? 3. : min(floor(...), 2.)`);
  per-bar levels are now 0.06 (level 0) and ~0.22-0.26 (others), peak 0.87.
- **Mix balanced to the 8K's** (the owner liked that music): A-weighted rms
  per instrument in the full sections (`tools/stems.py`, same tool on both):
  kick .035, snare .025, bass .020, pad .019, arp .014, hook .039 (the 8K:
  .035, .025, .020, .017, .012, .040); the full mix .069 with the hook. The
  first 4K mix had the hook 8 dB too loud and the bass 8 dB too quiet.
- Levels and clicks per instrument: `S=... python3 tools/stems.py src/synth.frag`.
  The arpeggio's "clicks" are its bright pluck's 3 ms attack (the 8K's
  arpeggio is flagged the same way); the other instruments are clean.

## 6. Size budget

The two shaders compressed together went from 3026 B (the first 4K
version, Linux 4509 B) to about 2600 B. The main steps, with what they saved when
they were measured (both shaders, LZMA; the numbers interact a little):

| Change | Saved |
|---|---|
| visuals rewrite: one camera path from bar 30 through `fly()` instead of three shots, the bulb lit by the scene's sun, forward-difference normal, the camera matrix reused for the tesseract's projection, gamma 2 | 200 |
| music: chords as a formula (no 36-entry table), pad without the crossfade loop, small golfs | 57 |
| tesseract: one 4D rotation (13), one edge colour (12) | 25 |
| ground kept (no fade special case) | 21 |
| crash cymbal removed | 16 |
| hook without its one rest, hook check by the current level | 14 |
| soft shadow without early exit | 10 |
| identifier names (`tools/namesearch.py`, three rounds, on the payload) | ~48 |
| many small golfs (vertex signs, `sph()` in the bulb, merged cosines, ...) | ~60 |

Still possible if bytes are needed (measured at the time, approximate):
noise risers 40, booms 43, constant 16th arpeggio 21, soft shadow 46, lit
rooms 55, blueprint grid 44, Linux without the adaptive resolution 119.
Restoring a feature costs about the same: crash 16, edge colours 12, the
second 4D rotation 13. After any shader change run `tools/namesearch.py`
again (it keeps the names it knows and searches from there; ~5-10 min).

## 7. Testing (commands)

```sh
S=/path/to/scratch            # with the preview binary: gcc -O2 -o $S/preview ../tools/preview.c -lGL -lX11 -lm
./build.sh                    # all three, checks the size; runs check_shaders.sh when $S/preview exists
tools/check_shaders.sh        # music bit-identical source/minified/core, frames identical (compat)
python3 tools/flypath.py      # camera clearance and turn rates (numpy); "design" refits h(t)
python3 tools/stems.py src/synth.frag     # per-instrument levels, clicks
python3 tools/bench.py src/scene.frag     # frame cost per moment
python3 tools/namesearch.py 420 1 /tmp/names1.json   # then copy the best to tools/names.json
```

Platform runs (scratch scripts, same as for the 8K): each build plays the
whole song under Xvfb (1280x720) with the audio recorded from a PipeWire null
sink, screenshots at fixed times; the recording is compared with the
reference render of `check_shaders.sh` (`rz_b.wav`) window by window. The
macOS build runs through `../tools/macsim` (assemble `src/mac/main.asm` as
ELF with `-D_dlopen=dlopen -D_dlsym=dlsym -D_main=main -DFWDIR=...` and the
fake frameworks; see `../tools/macsim/build.sh`, with `-I rozmery4k/src/`).

## 8. Performance

Frame times with Mesa llvmpipe at 480x270 (CPU, 4 threads; only the ratios
matter): building 18 ms, tesseract 13 ms, bulb on the grid 33 ms, dive 94
ms, flight 100-177 ms, heaviest bars 94-95 (~198 ms, the climb over the
formation); `T_HEAVY` = 182.4 s (bar 95) measures there. The soft shadow
without early exit costs +26 % on the bulb's birth (cheap frames) and nothing
at the heaviest ones.

NVIDIA rules (see `../HANDOFF.md`, section 2): no int arguments in overloaded
builtins (only `max(int, int)` here, an exact match), smoothstep edges always
increasing, no negative modulo or array index, no normalize of a zero vector
(the camera's vectors were checked analytically along the path).
